How to use MeshRadio

MeshRadio plays the songs people share each day on the Austin MeshCore #music channel, against that day's theme.

  • Now Playing — ▶ / ⏸ play or pause, ⏭ skip, 🔀 shuffle what's up next, drag the bar to scrub, and set the volume. It always shows the latest day, rolling forward on its own when a new day's songs start arriving.
  • Queue — the songs up next. Select a track to reveal ⤒ Play next and ✕ Remove in the bar up top; Clear queue empties it.
  • Export — ⤴ Export opens the whole day's songs as a YouTube playlist (every song for the day, whatever's playing).
  • Archive — a calendar of every day the channel played; tap a lit day to play or browse it. ▶ Play this day replays a whole day; + queue adds a single track. ☰ All themes swaps the calendar for the list of every theme the channel has run, so you can see what's been done before.
  • Keep playing — 🎲 Keep playing never lets the music stop: when the queue empties it keeps going with random songs from the archive.

  • Playback — songs stream from YouTube right in your browser, and your queue is your own — playing or skipping never affects anyone else.
